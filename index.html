Our Scope of Operation

Our Approach to Keeping You Insured

Leadfort Insurance Brokers Limited deploys every expertise at its disposal in carrying out the job for which it was engaged by clients. our scope of operation covers such areas as: 

Risk Evaluation

We will analyze and evaluate your loss exposure with the objective of discerning the maximum probable financial loss that the organization may suffer should any risk occur. Here, we would deploy every conceivable risk management tool in carrying out this function

Risk Identification

Leadfort will study all the processes/ procedures you adopt or employ in your operations. The essence of this exercise is to unravel the various areas in which your organization can suffer any form of loss, most especially financial loss.

Risk Handling Measures

We shall recommend the most plausible risk handling techniques that would mitigate the loss occurrence. Or at least reduce the financial loss that may follow such an occurrence

24/7 Fast Support

We ensure that you never have to face an unexpected challenge alone. Our dedicated team of experienced professionals is always available to provide prompt assistance whenever you need it.
