About Our Company

Leadfort Insurance Brokers Limited “Leadfort” is a Nigerians wholly owned company, with corporate head office at 14A Pilot’s Court, Bosun Adekoya Street, Oniru Extension, Lekki Phase 1, Lekki, Lagos. The company was established to develop, package and deliver quality and highly innovative insurance products that best satisfy customer needs, whilst operating a highly ethical, efficient, resourceful and profitable organization that will transcend the expectation of all stakeholders in wealth creation. We believe that the extent and depth of our service delivery, fine-tuned to the peculiarity of each customer, and coupled with the trusting relationships we have built with our clients form the basis of our strength in insurance that rises above the times.

OUR CORPORATE PHILOSOPHY

Leadfort strongly emphasizes on having a long term productive relationship of mutual respect and trust with all our clients. We pay attention to every detailed risk situation and respond swiftly to every claim and/or every situation requiring our attention thereby protecting the wealth and investments of our clients. We deploy all our expertise and exceptional degree of personal attention in carrying out our functions/duties. We never forget that every client is unique with differential problems and specific needs. In pursuance of this, we always rely on the strength of our in-house expertise and highly valued crop of creative professionals, and this, enable us guaranty the provision of services of the highest possible standards.

VISION STATEMENT

To be the leading insurance and reinsurance broking firm.

MISSION STATEMENT

To build a risk management organization in insurance and reinsurance broking while guaranteeing good returns and upholding the tenets and practices of the industry.

OUR VALUES

Professionalism and excellence anchored by mutual respect and trust in ensuring a value driven brand.

WHY LEADFORT INSURANCE BROKERS LIMITED?

1. Choice of Insurance Company:

Since we have extensive knowledge of the market, we ensure the placement of our client’s risk with financially strong and reputable insurance company at competitive terms.

2. Claims Administration:

We ensure that our client’s claims are promptly and equitably settled by making prompt follow-ups and early resolution of conflict if any.

3. Portfolio Management:

A periodic review of our client’s insurances is carried out to ensure full coverage at all times, as their business expands and the nature of their operation increases their risk exposure.

4. Cost Management:

Considering the increase in cost of operation, we manage our client’s insurance portfolio with a view to bringing down insurance costs through negotiation for a reduced rate and necessary discounts as applicable using our professional know-how.
